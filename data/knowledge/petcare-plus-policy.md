# PetCare Plus Policy Fixture

This is a synthetic policy fixture for demos and tests. It is not a real insurance product.

## Clause 12: Deductible

For eligible inpatient treatment, the insurer subtracts the deductible stated in the policy schedule before calculating the reimbursable claim amount.

## Clause 18: Waiting Period

Illness treatment that starts during the waiting period is not eligible for reimbursement unless the policy schedule explicitly provides an exception.

## Clause 24: Covered Disease Scope

Only diseases listed in the covered disease schedule are eligible for illness claim reimbursement.

## Clause 31: Accident Definition

An accident means a sudden, external, unintended event that directly causes bodily injury to the insured pet.
